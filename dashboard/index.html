How this radar works

Every protocol in the snapshot is read from DefiLlama's record. A protocol is on the board when its record carries no token — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no ticker — and it is not a centralised exchange, a versioned fork (a name ending in V2, v2 or 2.0), or a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. The board ranks them by an implied value:

implied = TVL × base multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Base multiple — what a dollar of TVL is worth as token value, by what the protocol does. Lending 0.35, derivatives and perpetuals 0.40, DEXs 0.30, yield 0.25, RWA and staking 0.20, bridges 0.15, everything else 0.25. Lending and derivatives monetise every dollar directly; a bridge moves value through without keeping it.
  • Growth — 1 + clamped 7-day TVL change × 0.8. A +120% week lifts the multiple to 1.96; a −60% week cuts it to 0.52. Wild weeks are clamped so one spike cannot carry the board.
  • Maturity — 0.5 + up to 0.5 for up to four years tracked. A protocol that has run for years with no token has survived audits, cycles and competitors.
  • Breadth — 1 + 0.05 per extra chain, up to 1.20. TVL spread across chains is stickier than TVL on one chain.

Implied value is capped at twice TVL and rounded to whole dollars. Ties break by slug, so the ranking is identical on every run.

What the snapshot is and is not

The snapshot is DefiLlama's record, fetched once. Records no token is not the same as has no token — a protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry. The board says what a token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued.

The watchlist is a floor, never a ceiling: it holds the 40 protocols at or above $50M of TVL that meet the house rule. The board ranks every rankable pre-token protocol in the snapshot, including ones below that floor — the floor is where the reading started, not where it stops.

Farm plans

Each protocol carries eight steps: a fresh wallet, the category's own action with a capital tier from its TVL ($50–$250), one qualifying action a week, a hold of 30–60 days, checking the protocol's own points page, claiming only from links the protocol publishes, logging everything, and an exit plan. Every step states what it costs and how long it takes.
